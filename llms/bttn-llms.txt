# bttn

> bttn (bttn Inc., trading online as bttnusa.com) is a Seattle, Washington based
> business-to-business e-commerce marketplace and wholesale distributor of medical
> supplies, lab equipment and pharmaceuticals, serving clinics, dental, veterinary and
> physical-therapy practices, clinical laboratories and home-healthcare providers across
> the United States. Founded in 2021 by JT Garwood and Jack Miller and backed by Tiger
> Global, it runs a self-serve storefront of 60,000+ catalog products with bulk pricing,
> same- or next-business-day shipping, quotes, purchase-order/invoice payment and
> recurring reorder tooling.

## APIs

bttn publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-08 there is no developer portal, no API
reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no MCP server and no A2A agent card on
any bttn host. Buyer integration (bulk, recurring, or purchase-order ordering) is handled
through the storefront and the sales team rather than a documented programmable interface.

## Notes for agents

- The host `www.bttnusa.com` answers **HTTP 200 with an HTML catch-all page for every
  unknown path**. Do not infer that a document exists from a 200 alone; diff the body
  against a control path that cannot exist.
- `bttn.com`, `bttn.io` and `bttn.co` are **not** bttn Inc. properties — a parked
  domain-broker page, a redirect to Button Inc. (usebutton.com), and BTTN Holdings LLC
  respectively.
